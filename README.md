# MediBridge & LabEase
### An All-in-One Patient Health Companion

> Making medical information easier to understand, health records easier to organize, and everyday health management more accessible.

MediBridge & LabEase is a patient-centered digital health companion that integrates laboratory report interpretation, medical record organization, women's health tracking and medication management into one platform.

---

## The Problem

Patients often struggle to understand laboratory reports filled with medical terminology and unfamiliar biomarker values. Medical records are frequently scattered across different platforms, making it difficult to compare results and monitor changes over time.

Women's health tracking, medication adherence and diagnostic information are also often managed separately, creating a fragmented healthcare experience.

## Our Solution

MediBridge & LabEase brings four healthcare-support modules together into one integrated experience.

| Module | Purpose |
|---|---|
| LabEase | Simplifies laboratory report interpretation. |
| MediBridge Vault | Organizes medical records and supports longitudinal tracking. |
| Adaptive Women's Health Suite | Supports menstrual-cycle, BBT and hormone-related tracking. |
| MediTrack | Helps manage daily medication schedules and adherence. |

---

## Core Modules

### 1. LabEase — Lab Report Intelligence

Designed to make laboratory information more understandable for patients.

- OCR and regex-based report parsing concept.
- Color-coded biomarker range visualization.
- Bilingual explanations in English and Hinglish.
- Patient-friendly interpretation of laboratory values.
- Doctor-question checklists to encourage informed consultations.

### 2. MediBridge Vault — Longitudinal Medical Records

A centralized concept for organizing health information and reviewing previous results.

- Organized medical report archive.
- Historical biomarker tracking.
- Side-by-side report comparison.
- Longitudinal health data organization.

### 3. Adaptive Women's Health Suite

A women's health module designed around customizable cycle tracking and biomarker awareness.

- Customizable cycle length: 21–45 days.
- Customizable bleeding duration: 2–10 days.
- Menstrual-cycle phase and hormone-status tracking.
- Basal Body Temperature (BBT) tracking.
- Correlation with hormonal laboratory panels.

### 4. MediTrack — Medication & Adherence

A daily medication-management module designed to support routine adherence.

- Daily prescription management.
- Medication time slots.
- Dose-completion tracking.
- Adherence monitoring.
- Follow-up appointment synchronization concept.
